ALL ABOUT ME

My name is Akina Salazar. I'm a designer based in San Francisco, originally from Santa Clara, California. I'm currently studying design at the University of San Francisco. I've been designing my whole life whether that was drawing Pokémon as a kid, or creating posters for my favorite bands in the present. Music is one of my main inspirations for designs. Some of my favorite artists include Fontaines D.C., Arctic Monkeys, and Rehash. When I'm not designing you can find me playing my guitar, drawing, or skating.

MACBOOK

HEADPHONES

One time I went on BART and I forgot my headphones.. Never again. I am always listening to music whether that's when I'm designing, walking, drawing, doing homework, or on the bus. My favorite artists have influenced me in ways that have changed my life. These artists include but are not limited to: Fontaines D.C., Arctic Monkeys, Dominic Fike, and Rehash. I also have a pair of headphones I plug into my guitar amp so I don't annoy my roommate when playing.

ELECTRIC GUITAR

When I'm not listening to music, I'm playing the actual music with my guitar. I started playing the summer before my junior year in high school and I've never looked back since. Playing guitar has opened my eyes to a huge community of people who play live music. I've met many friends through playing together and even got some of my current friends into playing an instrument. This is my second electric guitar, it's a Fender Telecaster.

AMPLIFIER

An electric guitar cannot sound cool without an amplifier. This is what you plug your guitar into to amplify the sound and add cool effects like reverb and distortion if your amp comes with those selections. I picked the orange amp brand because I saw it being used onstage by Oasis and Arctic Monkeys. I also loved the color and how when I played live there was a huge orange block behind me.

GUITAR PEDALS

My guitar pedals are a recent addition to my guitar accessory collection. Nobody told me before starting this hobby that I'm going to spend so much on extra stuff. Pedals affect the sound of your guitar. I have a distortion pedal alongside a pedal machine with a built in looper and many adjustable settings to achieve the tone I want. Pedals are great because you could control them with your foot while playing live to have a seamless transition from one tone to another.

LITTLE GUITAR TUNER

My friend Sophia put me onto this small guitar tuner. It clips to the headstock of your guitar and has a sneaky display you can look at at the back of your guitar. It uses the sound waves within your guitar to identify notes, so you don't even need to be audible to tune. Great for playing live when you don't wanna annoy an audience with the open notes of a guitar.

GUITAR CABLES

Nothing would be connected to each other without cables. The cables pictured here include the chord to power my amp, the cable that connects my amp to my guitar, and an aux chord. The aux chord allows me to connect my phone to my amp to play along to my favorite songs.

CHARGERS

Pictured here is the charger for my phone and for my mac. None of this would be possible without electricity and a full battery!

FLASHDRIVE

As a designer I like to store some of my designs in a flash drive for easy transportation between computers. It's so cool to have a physical storage of my designs that I can hold and say “my designs are in here.” I use this most often for design projects for class because the printers only work with school desktops, so I need to transfer my files from my computer to the desktops.

MOUSE

Using a track pad for designing can become annoying because it's so small. A mouse allows for broader distance for your mouse to travel and an overall more comfortable experience. I also use this mouse when I play minecraft with my friends

ADAPTER

This adapter allows various technological trinkets to work with my mac. Mac is designed so that it's not compatible with many ports (very frustrating), so oftentimes people need an adapter. I use mine mostly for my mouse and an audio interface when playing guitar. If you look very closely, all the tech I use on a daily basis is all connected with each other in some way.
